# Ichigo expansion and upper tiers

Six exact forms are added to the existing 85-card proposal. These are NIGHT
editorial grades within BLEACH, not official grades or cross-universe scores.

| Form | Grade | Design | Continuity |
|---|---|---|---|
| Human / everyday Ichigo | F | Vanguard | Anime |
| Mastered Hollow mask | S | Emberglass | Anime |
| Full Hollow / Ulquiorra battle | S | Emberglass | Anime |
| Dangai-trained Ichigo | SS | Obsidian | Anime |
| Final Getsuga Tensho / Mugetsu | SSS | Eclipse | Anime |
| Blood Chains Ichigo | EX | Ascendant | Anime |

Eclipse uses curved pearl ribs and an indigo field. Ascendant uses an offset,
sculpted opening, pale porcelain, dark metal and restrained red seams. Both retain
the smaller name and the top-right glass letters without a background plate.
Actual character contours and weapons cross the inner structural opening.

Blood Chains is the Kubo-designed and named anime form announced on the official
site on 2026-08-22. Its official keyword profile documents the further
transformation after Hollow awakening and Bankai. The EX placement is a proposal;
the qualitative description is not translated into a universal destruction feat.
It is not substituted for the manga's True Bankai.

SSS and EX evidence now binds to form ID and original source SHA as well as
universe and continuity. Opaque posters require a source-bound matte with its own
checksum and crop. The original RGB, bytes, dimensions and rights remain recorded.
The saved mattes refine Human Ichigo’s white shirt, Mugetsu’s blue hair and
bandages, and Blood Chains’ coloured silhouette against adjacent/background
pixels. These change alpha only; the saved raster masks are pinned beside the
outline guides.

Remaining catalogue entries still await suitable matching artwork: completed
Fullbring, Fullbring-enhanced Bankai, True Shikai, manga True Bankai, Horn of
Salvation, and Hell Verse empowerment. No card is produced by relabelling an
unrelated form. The film continuity remains separate.

Validation: unit tests, 3000 × 4800 exports, native-pixel crops, 320px proofs,
rank bounds and physical frame crossing. New reviews are recorded only after
inspection. Existing accepted website images retain their original bytes and
release provenance; changing the renderer does not renew their approval.

# Proposed tier arrangement

Working arrangement for review, 2026-10-19. The grade follows the pictured form within BLEACH, then selects its visual design. Naruto will receive independent assessments. Existing letters remain editorial proposals, not official rankings or a completed feat audit.

| Rank | Proposed design | Visual identity | Acquired cards |
| --- | --- | --- | ---: |
| F | Vanguard | Graphic typography, spare grey architecture | 0 |
| E | Ivory | Pale mineral field, soft silver edge | 1 |
| D | Voltage | Acid accents, angular cuts and diagonal type | 3 |
| C | Crimson | Muted oxblood, dark steel and italic identity | 5 |
| B | Tidelight | Pearl field, curved asymmetric architecture | 18 |
| A | Nocturne | Blue-black depth, sculpted metal frame | 41 |
| S | Emberglass | Amber folds, warm metal, layered light | 16 |
| SS | Obsidian | Black lacquer, copper architecture | 1 |
| SSS | Open | Needs its own design and specific feat evidence | 0 |
| EX | Open, above SSS | Needs a distinct exceptional design and evidence | 0 |

The seven occupied styles are shown together in the review sheet. F stays empty until suitable human Ichigo artwork is acquired; no transformed illustration is relabelled to fill it. Parallax is excluded.

The same character/form retains one grade across different poses. Every active edition has a distinct original source SHA. Both acquired Kenpachi forms are currently A and therefore Nocturne in this proposal. The separate Crimson Kenpachi file is a material/depth comparison with the real A letter preserved; it is not a C-grade Kenpachi or another active edition.

Every design must show the physical figure or weapon crossing the inner frame. The outer card edge remains print trim. Subdued aura inside the opening cannot substitute for real silhouette overlap. Glass rank glyphs float directly over the card without a badge container. Names retain the accepted 84% scale.

The offline gallery combines search and a tier filter. This proposal does not change drop rates, trading prices, bot publication or live deployment. See [rank rules](POWER-RANKS.md), [per-form boundaries](COLLECTION-REVIEW.md) and [validation](COLLECTION-VALIDATION.md).

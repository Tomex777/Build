# BLEACH collection review

The active collection contains 85 editions across 76 characters. Each edition has a distinct original illustration and a form-bound proposed power grade. The seven replacement illustrations come from the official BLEACH anime, BLEACH: Brave Souls and BLEACH: Soul Resonance publications.

## Composition

The accepted Yoruichi Nocturne matte is preserved. Ten additional aura-heavy portraits now use source-bound character mattes: Gin, Ginjo, Hisagi, Kaien, Kensei, Mashiro, Rangiku, Chad, Ulquiorra and Unohana. The subdued aura sits inside the architectural opening; the figure and weapon silhouette sit in front of the frame. Six further sources now have foreground mattes: Byakuya, Hirako, Isane, Kenpachi, Riruka and Stark. The matte changes alpha only; retained RGB values come from the pinned original. No generated artwork pixels are used. The archived renderer remains unchanged.

Seven source records have distinct replacement illustrations: Aizen Muken, Byakuya Senbonzakura, Kenpachi TYBW, Rukia TYBW, Yoruichi Flash Step, and the two restored-Shikai Ichigo poses. Their active design now follows the proposed tier mapping. Per-design crop/placement keeps faces visible and avoids filling the entire frame with aura. Glass rank letters and the 84% title scale are preserved.

## Forms and grades

Ranks compare forms within BLEACH and the recorded continuity, independently of Naruto or any other universe. Existing letters remain proposals; this visual pass does not claim a new exhaustive feat audit.

Every acquired source was checked against its source/profile with an explicit scope and exclusion boundary. Grades remain NIGHT editorial proposals, not official statistics or an exhaustive chapter-by-chapter feat audit. A later uniform alone does not establish later training or an active transformation. The same character/form keeps the same grade across poses and finishes.

The two restored-Shikai Ichigo illustrations are A. Early Substitute Ichigo is D, original Shikai is B, and original Bankai is A. These broad bands do not assert that different A-grade forms are identical or guarantee a matchup result. Human Ichigo remains F in pending data until matching artwork is acquired. No current portrait is forced into SSS.

Ginjo's bone armour is the enhanced Fullbring, not his earlier training state. Ikkaku's chained giant axe and Kensei's armoured fists are Bankai. Standard Quincy portraits do not receive Vollstandig, Yhwach does not receive awakened Almighty or Soul King absorption, and sealed Royal Guard portraits do not receive blood-oath release.

## Pending art

The duplicate Ulquiorra Tidelight edition is pending a distinct unreleased-form illustration. Missing Ichigo transformations remain pending and unrendered. A stronger illustration must not be relabelled as human or another weaker form.

## Gallery

The gallery is ordered strongest-first and filters by character/form and tier. Each proposed tier has one design, so a separate finish filter is redundant. The card viewer includes source links and the exact reasoning. `gallery` exports one offline HTML with embedded images. The publication path still requires current full-resolution visual reviews; these source/form reviews do not automatically approve visual masters.

```bash
npm ci
npm test
npm run build -- --out dist/bleach-refined-masters
npm run gallery -- --out dist/bleach-refined-masters --file dist/Bleach-Collection.html
```

The renderer needs pinned Chrome for Testing 131.0.6778.204; use NIGHT_BROWSER_PATH if its executable lives outside .cache/browser. Existing visual approvals are invalidated when source, form, crop, rank, masks or renderer change. No live bot deployment is part of this work.

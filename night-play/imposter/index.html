FIELD GUIDE

YOU CAN'T TRUST ANYONE.

Move: WASD, arrow keys, tap a destination, or use the touch joystick.

Use: Stand near a glowing console and press E or USE. Repair three stations and investigate security archives.

Kill: As imposter, approach an operative and press K or KILL. Non-graphic takedowns leave evidence.

Report: Find a downed operative and press R or REPORT. Accuse suspects in the ensuing meeting.

Meet: Press M or MEET. You have limited emergency calls; the computer players can vote too.

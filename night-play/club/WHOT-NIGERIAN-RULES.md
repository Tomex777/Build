# NIGHT Play WHOT — researched rules and selectable modes
Updated 16 October 2026. This is an **unofficial, independent adaptation**.

## Core Nigerian game mechanics
- A traditional 54-card WHOT pack has five shapes: Ball/Circle, Angle/Triangle, Cross, Carpet/Square, Star; plus five WHOT 20 cards. The NIGHT deck inventory remains exact.
- A turn matches the played card's **number or shape**, or plays WHOT 20 to request any new shape; otherwise the player picks from the **Market**.
- Standard action defaults: **1 Hold On** (same player again); **2 Pick Two**; **5 Pick Three**; **8 Suspension**; **14 General Market** (others draw); **20 Crown/WHOT** (call a shape).
- When the market runs out, shuffle the played cards except the visible top card into a new market.
- Star cards count double *if using numeric penalty points*. NIGHT's current Quick Play and first-to-two Tournament do not use numeric penalty points.

## Finish/win is genuinely variable by table
Basic published WHOT rules usually award a round to the first player whose hand is empty.
This must **not** be silently treated as the only Nigerian house rule:
- **NIGHT Strict Check Up (ON by default):** a final special/action card **1, 2, 5, 8, 14, or 20** does not immediately win. In particular **5 Square** (Pick Three) triggers its action, but the player must **pick one from Market** before play continues. The opponent still owes any pick penalty (which may be defended).
- **Strict Check Up OFF:** standard first-empty-hand win, including ending on an action card.
- **Last Card declaration (OFF by default):** when enabled, announce before playing the final card; failing costs two market cards. The CPU announces automatically.
- **Defence (ON by default):** a Pick Two can be returned with another **2**, and Pick Three with another **5**. Stacks compound to **4/6/etc.**, and players unable to defend draw the accumulated penalty. The computer plays by the same options.
- Special-card effects and victory priority differ across regions and apps; NIGHT explicitly labels these choices as *house settings*.

## NIGHT-created modes (not canonical WHOT ranks)
- **Quick Play:** one round, CPU.
- **Career:** persistent wins, losses and streaks. Promote after every three wins: Kindergarten → Primary → Secondary → Academy → Rookie → Soldier → Corporal → Sergeant → Captain → Major → General. This is NIGHT's own game progression, not an externally verified WHOT hierarchy. Higher career stages improve the CPU when difficulty is Normal.
- **Tournament:** first player to win two rounds against the CPU; a completed series resets on replay. It is not the 3-AI-player elimination tournament offered by some other products.

5. Reddit /r/Nigeria player experiences: https://www.reddit.com/r/Nigeria/comments/1arnt0i — players disagree about Last Card / Check Up conventions.
6. In a separate community WHOT game, cards **1, 8, 14 and 20** cannot finish a round: https://yasminesterea.com/ — supports the principle that *no-win card sets vary* by implementation. NIGHT also includes **2 and 5** to match its requested strict variation.

Future options worth research before implementation: multi-card plays of the same number, selectable suit-specific blocking, score-based elimination tournament, multiplayer that connects real people on different devices. **Do not label local pass-and-play as online.**

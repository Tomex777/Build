Terms of use

NIGHT Play preview · Updated 13 October 2026

Using the preview

NIGHT Play is currently a testing preview. Games, scores, and features may change. The preview is offered without guarantees of continuous availability.

Scores and fair play

Scores saved on your device are not official rankings. Please avoid manipulating other players’ games, accounts, or matches. Competitive multiplayer is not yet available in this preview.

Your account

WhatsApp account linking has not launched. Do not enter WhatsApp passwords, pairing codes, or private session credentials into this website. When accounts launch, the terms will be updated to describe the service.

Contact and changes

These are provisional preview terms, not the final terms for a commercial launch. An official contact address and final legal review must be added before the public account service launches.

Privacy

NIGHT Play preview · Updated 13 October 2026

What this preview saves

The games use your browser’s local storage for settings, high scores, recent game results, and whether you dismissed an announcement. These records stay in that browser unless you clear its storage or the site’s implementation changes.

WhatsApp and identity

The preview has no working WhatsApp account-linking system. It does not currently receive your WhatsApp phone number, JID, LID, or credentials through a sign-in flow. Do not submit account secrets here.

Future services

Before launching verified accounts, multiplayer, or hosted game statistics, NIGHT Play will need a revised policy covering server storage, identity linking, retention, and account removal.

Third-party hosting

The website is hosted through Vercel. Hosting infrastructure may process technical request information independently of the game records stored in your browser.

Updates

This page describes the current test build, not a final production privacy policy. It must be reviewed and completed before live account registration is enabled.

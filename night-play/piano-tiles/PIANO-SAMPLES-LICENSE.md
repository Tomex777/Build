NIGHT Play redistributes selected, unmodified MP3 recordings of this grand piano, self-hosted in `piano-tiles/samples/`. We apply pitch resampling, dynamic gain envelopes and light Web Audio mastering at playback time. There is no claim of affiliation or endorsement by Alexander Holm, Tone.js, or the original recording project.

The sample recordings retain their CC BY 3.0 license. They are **not** relicensed as part of NIGHT Play's application code. Keep this notice when reusing or distributing the recordings.

Samples present: C3, Fs3, A3, C4, Ds4, Fs4, A4, C5, Ds5, Fs5.

**Why these recordings?** The earlier built-in triangle oscillator produced a synthetic ringtone-like tone. The replacement instrument plays real piano recordings with actual hammer/string timbre and naturally decaying harmonics; long notes retain the sample tail and decay on key release.

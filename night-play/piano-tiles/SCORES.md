# Complete piano scores

The two full-score game arrangements retain every note-on event, including
simultaneous bass and treble notes, from these Mutopia MIDI files. One tile
plays one onset/chord. The game's equal tile-height gaps adapt the pacing for
four-lane interaction; these are not recordings at the source performance tempo.

| Work | Source / typesetter | License |
| --- | --- | --- |
| J. S. Bach, Prelude in C major, BWV 846 | [Mutopia 5](https://www.mutopiaproject.org/cgibin/piece-info.cgi?id=5), Tobias Erbsland | Public Domain |
| L. V. Beethoven, Für Elise, WoO 59 | [Mutopia 931](https://www.mutopiaproject.org/cgibin/piece-info.cgi?id=931), Stelios Samelis; Breitkopf & Härtel 1888 | Public Domain |

Original MIDI files are preserved in `scores/`. `full-scores.js` includes source
SHA-256 hashes and the complete grouped note events. Instrument recordings have
their separate CC BY 3.0 attribution in `PIANO-SAMPLES-LICENSE.md`.

The rest of the library distinguishes complete familiar melodies, excerpts,
original NIGHT studies, and endless mode. A melody is not represented as the
entire classical composition from which it comes.

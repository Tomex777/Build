# Chromium Dino engine used by NIGHT Play

The **game engine**, original jump physics, collision boxes and run/duck frame
timing originate from the Chromium Dino / T-Rex Runner project, licensed under
BSD-3-Clause. © Chromium Authors (2014 onward).

Our vendored JavaScript began from the standalone extraction at
https://github.com/wayou/t-rex-runner (index.js).
Original upstream: https://chromium.googlesource.com/chromium/src/+/main/components/neterror/resources/dino_game/

`LICENSE.chromium.txt` preserves the applicable Chromium BSD-3-Clause notice.
The original sprite atlas is used **only as an internal layout template**
(including clouds, horizon and other shared engine atlas regions).

**NIGHT Play-specific artwork** is generated by `night-skin.js`, replacing
the visible T-Rex frames with a mint-armoured courier, cactus frames with
crystalline growths, and pterodactyl frames with winged sentries.
All artwork in the new character and obstacle regions was authored for NIGHT
Play. The sprite placements retain the original Chromium engine's source
frame widths/heights so collision and jump calculations are unaffected.

Other adaptations to vendored engine code:
- Remove Chromium's full-window arcade-mode zoom and automatic boot.
- Keep the gameplay's 150-unit vertical physics and original frame rates,
  while adjusting logical viewport width and rendering it with a uniform
  magnification so the actual game fills the 390:550 NIGHT Play arena.
- Introduce flying sentries sooner by lowering their speed threshold;
  obstacle physics, hitboxes and their random-generator logic remain native.

NIGHT Play's native interface, swipe-down duck gesture (with no dock button),
sound settings, personal bests, game pause, and responsive framing are in
`bridge.js` and `style.css`.

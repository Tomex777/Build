Slumber 0.8.12 — production audio conversion UI

Base: successful Slumber 0.8.11 audio-engine artifact from Actions run 34955720071.
Branch: pianohub-ci only. main untouched.

Adds:
- real "Convert audio to lesson" action in My XMLs
- Android OpenDocument audio picker with persistable read permission
- persistent WorkManager polling so jobs remain visible after leaving/reopening Songs
- user-facing stages are sourced directly from AudioLessonWorker progress
- stage percentages are shown only when the worker reports a real completed/total measurement
- active jobs can be cancelled
- failed/cancelled jobs can be retried using locally persisted URI/display-name metadata
- failed jobs can be dismissed without leaving dead UI cards
- successful conversion refreshes My XMLs and reports the saved lesson title
- honest production copy: piano/instrument-focused recordings are strongest; busy full mixes may be less reliable
- no lyric claims for audio transcription
- versionCode 24 / versionName 0.8.12

The conversion result is saved through SlumberChartRepository as canonical local XML, so it immediately uses the existing Listen/Learn/Play Along/Perform flow and progress system.
